## 🎯 Target Customer Profiles

### Free Plan
- **Who**: Hobbyists, bloggers, single-person operations
- **Use Case**: Testing the platform, basic popup needs
- **Conversion Goal**: Upgrade when they hit limits or need CRM

### Starter Plan ($29/mo)
- **Who**: Small businesses, startups, freelancers
- **Use Case**: Lead generation, basic CRM, small websites
- **Typical Customer**: 1-5 employee companies, $0-50K ARR
- **Value Prop**: Full feature access at affordable price

### Professional Plan ($79/mo) ⭐ **MOST POPULAR**
- **Who**: Growing businesses, marketing agencies, SaaS companies
- **Use Case**: Multi-site lead gen, AI-powered CRM, landing pages
- **Typical Customer**: 5-25 employee companies, $50K-500K ARR
- **Value Prop**: AI features, unlimited landing pages, white label

### Enterprise Plan ($199/mo)
- **Who**: Large corporations, agencies, high-volume businesses
- **Use Case**: Multiple brands, custom integrations, unlimited scale
- **Typical Customer**: 25+ employees, $500K+ ARR
- **Value Prop**: Unlimited everything, priority support, custom dev

## 📈 Pricing Rationale

### Why These Prices?

1. **Competitive Analysis**:
   - HubSpot Marketing Hub: $50-$3,200/mo
   - OptinMonster: $9-$49/mo (popups only)
   - Leadpages: $37-$239/mo (landing pages only)
   - Pipedrive CRM: $14-$99/mo (CRM only)
   
   **Your Platform** = All-in-one → Higher value justified

2. **Feature Richness**:
   - 590 popup templates
   - Full CRM with pipelines
   - Landing page builder
   - Form builder
   - AI-powered features (GPT-4o)
   - Advanced analytics
   
   → Premium pricing warranted

3. **AI Justification**:
   - OpenAI API costs ~$0.002-0.02 per request
   - 500 AI requests = $1-10 cost
   - Priced at $50/mo increase for AI = healthy margin

4. **Market Positioning**:
   - **Free**: Freemium model for acquisition
   - **Starter**: Entry point, minimal friction
   - **Professional**: Sweet spot for SMBs ($79 = psychological price point)
   - **Enterprise**: Premium tier, value-based pricing
